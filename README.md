Project Overview:
•	Khulisa Social Solutions
•	Khulisa Social Solutions is a multi-award-winning non-profit organization in South Africa which was founded inn1997 with its initial focus on implementing rehabilitation programme for young offenders and other marginalized individuals.
•	The NPO’s mission is to empower vulnerable children, youth, marginalized individuals, and underfunded communities to unlock their potential to its fullest extent, develop resilient pathways and acquire the skills necessary for sustainable futures.
•	Their primary audience is communities, NGOs, government agencies, corporations and funders to invest in their project.

Website Goals and Objectives:
•	To increase website traffic, draw investment from shareholders and the public, 
•	Web visitors increase by 25%, Volunteer registrations increase by 15%, Donations increase by 20%, Bounce rate decreases by 10%, Page views increase by 15%

Features and Functionality:
•	Homepage: To display an overview what the organization has to offer
•	Services page: This page displays the different types of development programs and programs offered by the organisation
. Impact page: A page for displaying the work the company has done over the years.
•	Donations page: A page that highlights methods to donate to the organisation
•	About us page: A page to display what the overall vision, objectives and methods to achieve those objectives
•	Contact page: A page to display method in contacting the NPO to donate or volunteer
