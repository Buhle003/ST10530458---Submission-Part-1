Early Childhood Development (ECD)

Discover how Khulisa Social Solutions works with individuals, families and communities to create positive and lasting change through our Early Childhood Development (ECD).

Vula Programme

Accomodating dialogue between children, teachers, parents, learners, and community members fosters conflict resolution, peace promotion, and understanding. This approach addresses conflict causes and promotes harmonious solutions in various settings.

Rock 'n Rhyme

A programme that brings together caregivers, parents and children in a harmonious learning experience.Through a wide array of nursery rhymes in different genres and languages, accompanied by outcome-based development activities, the program merges joyous musical exploration with educational benefits.

Let the Young Child Grow

This initiative assists with obtaining sponsorships for children from underprivileged families to access quality crèche education. Social workers provide progress feedback and parental support.

People Puppet Programme

The People Puppet Programme provides toddlers with opportunities to explore and express their emotions through puppet performances and storytelling.
