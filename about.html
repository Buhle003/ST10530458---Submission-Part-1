About Us

Changing Lives Through Every Opportunity.

Khulisa Social Solutions is an organisation dedicated to creating opportunities for positive and sustainable changes in the lives of individuals by working along side them and communities and supporting people to reach their full potential.

Who We Are

Working together to build stronger communities.

Khulisa Social Solutions is a non-profit organisation that began in 1997, with the intention of providing rehabilitation for young offenders. Across three decades, Khulisa has expanded its work to address broader social challenges, including youth development, inequality, violence, unemployment, and fractured trust between people, institutions and systems.

We believe that meaningful change happens when communities are given the resources, opportunities and support they need in order to success and to create a better future.

Through community initiatives, partnerships, volunteers and development programmes, we aim to create lasting social impact.

Our Mission

Khulisa's mission is to empower marginalized individuals, vulnerable children, youth and communities to unlock their fullest potential, acquire skills for sustainable futures and development.

Our Vision

A lifetime commit to ensuring a future where communities are empowered, connected and equipped to create positive change for generations to come.

Our Approach

We operate nationally, employing more than 180 staff across 18 offices and work in approximately 150 - 250 communities in collaboration with 350 NGO partners, impacting tens of thousands of people annually.

Using system-wide, ecosystem-based approachs that actively engage with communities, NGOs, government, and the private sector through shared-learning, implemention, co-design and dialougue.

Our Purpose

Supporting people where they live by bridging the gap between resource availability and individual backgrounds.
