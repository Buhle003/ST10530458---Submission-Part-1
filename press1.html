Eastern Cape NGO Programme Shows Measurable Gains in Grassroots Capacity

24 August 2026

Khulisa Social Solutions has highlighted progress made through an initiative supporting community-based organisations in the Eastern Cape.

Strengthening Community Organisations

Community-based organisations play an important role in responding to local needs and supporting vulnerable communities. Strengthening their organisational capacity can help them deliver services more effectively and sustainably.

The programme focused on building the skills and organisational capabilities needed by grassroots organisations to improve their operations and community impact.

Building Sustainable Capacity

Developing stronger governance, financial management and organisational systems can help community organisations become more resilient and better positioned to access opportunities and resources.

Looking Ahead

Continued investment in grassroots organisations remains important for supporting local development and creating opportunities within communities.
